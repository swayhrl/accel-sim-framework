# Open issues and decision boundaries

- `REQUIRES_REQUALIFICATION` for splitkv and combine means existing traceg
  payloads exist but accepted RTX4080/V1 replay evidence is pending. This
  review pack does not upgrade either to `REUSABLE_NOW`.
- `MISSING_SIM_TRACE` counts in the frontier are exact safe asset gaps, not
  authorization to use Node109. Later review should prioritize the BALANCED
  gaps using the published marginal mass table and reuse/requalification
  outcomes.
- Source observed S2 duration and shape recurrence do not prove cross context
  or other model family stability. Future scientific holdouts must avoid
  mechanism discovery target identities.
- The burden points and Native duration/CTA proxies are planning estimates,
  not observed simulator wall time. Capture and replay burden must be measured
  when those activities are separately authorized.
