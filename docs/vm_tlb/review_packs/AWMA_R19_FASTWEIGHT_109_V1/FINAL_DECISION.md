# Final decision

`R19_FASTWEIGHT_STRONG_SOFTWARE_SUFFICIENT`

The real prompt activates all five trained fast-weight layers and the dependent
consumer reads nonzero updated state. The exact two-chunk PyTorch closed form
removes the released path's `cat+cumsum` organization and improves the complete
five-layer local boundary from 2.079488 ms to 1.592320 ms (23.43%). Once the
updated matrices are ready, their consumer is no slower than the corresponding
base/no-write consumer. What remains is dense method-mandated math, not a
defensible state-update hardware residual.

This conclusion applies only to the frozen public reproduction artifact and
local RTX4080 boundary. It is not an official-paper-checkpoint result and does
not claim end-to-end TTT speedup or model quality.
