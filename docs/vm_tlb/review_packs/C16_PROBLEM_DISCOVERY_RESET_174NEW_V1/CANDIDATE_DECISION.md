The ten cards in `PROBLEM_CANDIDATE_CARDS.tsv` were assembled from three routes: accepted C16 observations (A), concrete LR12 experiment groups (B), and one-variable diagnostic counterfactuals (C). The seven requested dimensions were judged separately; no summed rank or fictitious percentage confidence is used.

No card satisfies **all five** problem gates (exact falsifiable question, legal observable on strong compiled path, matched strong software, worthwhile oracle/headroom, and a prospective STOP), plus a non-covered nearest-neighbor gap. In particular, PC03/PC04 are *parking-lot questions*, not BACKUP candidates and not 109 tasks. Decision: **0 PRIMARY, 0 BACKUP**, `NO_NEW_PROBLEM_READY_FOR_NATIVE_VALIDATION`. This is a legitimate outcome, not a claim that the field lacks problems.

The reset's governing lesson is not to lower a percentage threshold until something passes. First obtain a phenomenon and legal denominator in the mature runtime. A large local traffic or utilization number cannot stand in for exposed time; an idealized speedup that changes representation, route set or kernel is not a free oracle. Correctness and software-baseline identity precede a performance ranking.
