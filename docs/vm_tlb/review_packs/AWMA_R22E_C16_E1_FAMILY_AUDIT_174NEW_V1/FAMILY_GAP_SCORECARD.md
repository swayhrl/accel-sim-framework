Legal within-authority timing observations (AWQ/RAW_FP16 median ratio, not bitwidth causality): q_proj M1 `0.9801`, M256 `1.3045`; down_proj M1 `0.5011`, M256 `1.2758`; up_proj M1 `0.4002`, M256 `1.8688`. E-C also records RAW_BF16/RAW_FP16 dtype ratios, but finite rounding means not exact same numerical function. M1023 AWQ `1.872869` ms and M1024 AWQ `1.781394` ms are a path transition with changed M, not a matched same-work speedup. The NCU V2 up-proj counters show a larger M256 semantic-range traffic volume; they have no tensor attribution or paired kernel duration and do not establish a speedup or slowdown mechanism.

Classification: A = strict logical duplication, **net local response UNKNOWN**, with one negative/differently scoped oracle diagnostic. B = repeated *mixed shape interaction* across up/down, q M1 uncertain and M256 negative for AWQ relative to RAW_FP16; implementation effects confounded. Neither is simply a traffic-only response, neither is a clean hardware residual.
