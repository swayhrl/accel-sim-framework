# AWMA VLA RTC VJP boundary — final decision

**Primary label: `VLA_VJP_RESULT_MIXED_NEEDS_REVIEW`.** This is a scientific STOP, not architecture admission.

## Raw facts

1. The pinned upstream LeRobot RTC module produced identity-only VJP on all nine `v(x)=2x` CPU fixtures. The single permitted line-order repair restored the full `(1−2t)·error` analytic VJP on 9/9. This is recorded as `UPSTREAM_RTC_VJP_SEMANTIC_GAP_OBSERVED`; upstream-vs-repaired time is never an optimization result.
2. The pinned SmolVLA LIBERO checkpoint strictly loaded all **450,046,176** parameter values without fetching base VLM weights. Frozen episode A (0) supplied real state, two AV1 camera streams, and task language; episode B (1) was selected in metadata but no B observation values were selected, inspected or timed. The minimum shared Parquet/video files physically contain B; A ingestion was filtered to episode 0 and decoded only its first 31 video frames. The repaired real model performed 10 guided VJPs through action-expert q_proj and action output, with detached prefix and no parameter gradients. A first-chunk canary fixed legal delay=4 frames.
3. A0 complete observation-ready→committed-chunk timing on four A windows used 3 groups × (2 warmups+5 formal repeats), 60 formal samples. Guided-window median **231.912634 ms**, MAD **0.343504 ms**. Separate nested CUDA-event diagnosis showed approximately **98.88 ms** across 10 VJP calls and **91.30 ms** across 10 denoiser calls. These diagnostic durations are not the primary timing or additive whole-chunk proof.
4. Saved-tensor hooks reported **2,499,627,900 per-step storage-deduplicated logical bytes** accumulated over 10 VJPs, while the allocator peak increment was **65,785,344 bytes**. Neither is DRAM traffic. The target-only ideal state/lifetime headroom was `UNKNOWN`: necessary VJP arithmetic and state traffic overlap inside the measured VJP interval, and subtracting all ~99 ms would violate the contract.
5. The author-supported compile A1 was not executable on this exact RTC path (`CUDA misaligned address` during TorchDynamo first guided call); no alternative compile mode was scanned. The one frozen fixed-buffer/weight-reuse A1 passed all four FP32 outputs and 240 stepwise trajectory comparisons **exactly**, with 30 VJP and expert-backward hooks. Its 45 guided formal samples had median **232.688743 ms**, MAD **0.295641 ms**: no material improvement (median ratio −0.335% versus A0). Paired median A1−A0 was +0.771 ms, while unguided control also shifted +0.643 ms in sequential acquisition; the small sign is not a causal slowdown claim.
6. The single bounded NSYS capture-range attempt executed a numerically correct guided A1 chunk and logged all expected NVTX wrapper counts, but the profiler produced **no `.nsys-rep`**. We did not repeat the capture to stay within the one-capture budget. Consequently GPU launch/DRAM traffic and exact NCU kernel selector remain `COUNTER_UNAVAILABLE` / `SELECTOR_NOT_QUALIFIED`; no NCU job was run. No GPU memory-bottleneck or compute-bottleneck inference is made from saved-tensor sizes.

## Boundary judgment

The full network VJP is real and materially contributes to complete chunk readiness. The particular strong software reuse of repeated prefix padding/weights did not improve full-chunk time, but the experiment cannot isolate how much of the remaining VJP interval is mandatory math versus saved-state production, lifetime or reload. A defensible **>5% state-lifetime-only** complete-chunk residual was not established. Under the preregistered F6 gate, sealed episode B was therefore **not opened**, and no validation/architecture claim is made. `VLA_VJP_STRONG_SOFTWARE_SUFFICIENT`, `VLA_VJP_COMPUTE_DOMINANT_NO_MEMORY_ARCH_GAP`, and `VLA_VJP_STATE_LIFETIME_RESIDUAL_PRESENT` would each overstate this evidence; the mixed/review label preserves the concrete uncertainty.

No VLA mechanism, 174 simulation, CCE/Liger lane, alternate VLA model, extra profiler run, or new experiment was started. The raw/hash index and exact execution receipts are in this review pack.
