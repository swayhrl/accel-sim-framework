## Causal interpretation for this Goal

The literature establishes several distinct translation failure modes: capacity/reach, interference-driven rewalks, MSHR or walker queueing, remote wafer/IOMMU traversal, UVM residency/migration, and long-lived LLM weight versus growing KV competition. They require different observations. In particular, [DEPOT](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00486) shows that similar L2 miss counts can correspond to different causes and different benefit from a replacement mechanism. [SoftWalker](https://zoon17.github.io/pdfs/SoftWalker_micro25.pdf) and [cuPTW](https://maxkev1n.github.io/publications/sigmetrics-2026/3805633.pdf) address walker throughput under demonstrated queue pressure. A virtual-page count cannot establish that queue pressure.

The one direct LLM translation paper is Segmentation. Its model and synthetic long-context KV pressure support a specific weight/KV hypothesis, but its 2.51× IPC is a simulated result under its own paging, allocation and injected-KV assumptions. It is not a measured C16 headroom number. Wafer-scale HDPAT/Clover/RIPPLE and MCM ACOPT do not directly transfer to C16's single RTX 4080 capture.

The checked 2024–2026 near neighbors already cover large pages/contiguity, warp-pattern prefetch and MSHR compression, page-walk scaling, dead-entry protection, and weight segmentation. A future C16 claim must survive those controls and show time attributable to translation rather than ordinary data cache/streaming.
