# Holdout output governance — specified, not yet operational

`HOLDOUT_SEAL_MANIFEST.json` binds input identities for MP04 (A00 plus 15 B prompts), MP07 (A00) and MP08 (PG-19 excerpt). Those **inputs** and their SHA are public and frozen. No holdout *output* has been generated. The manifest deliberately says `INPUTS_FROZEN_OUTPUT_SEAL_UNPROVISIONED`; there is no pinned recipient certificate, encrypted-output inventory, trusted coordinator commit or discovery freeze receipt. Thus the consumer cannot truthfully claim the holdout is sealed/available, and MP04/07/08 are not execution-ready.

Operational procedure for a separately approved Stage B:

1. A coordinator outside the shared 109/174 execution accounts provisions an X.509 recipient certificate and keeps the private key outside both nodes. Pin public certificate SHA and OpenSSL version in a new coordination commit. The Stage B producer writes plaintext only to an isolated private ephemeral directory, encrypts each output as CMS AES-256 DER before any transfer to 164, then transfers **ciphertext only** and records its SHA. No plaintext or key goes to the shared worktree, 164 durable output root or Lane6 consumer.
2. Stage A ends and 174 writes a discovery freeze receipt containing `DQ_id`, phenomenon, sign, estimator, materiality threshold, STOP rule and exact MP04/07/08 holdout IDs. The coordinator commits this receipt and its SHA **before** Stage B outputs are opened. `HOLDOUT_SEAL_MANIFEST.json` is advanced to `SEALED_OUTPUTS_READY` only in a reviewed coordination commit with certificate SHA, ciphertext inventory, trusted commit and the receipt SHA.
3. `holdout_access_gate.py` rejects missing/untrusted receipt fields, wrong SHA, changed point set, plaintext path, missing ciphertext or bad ciphertext SHA. Its success only means **eligible for coordinator key release**, never that it decrypted or accepted a scientific result. The coordinator separately validates the trusted remote commit and releases decryption ability after this gate; the consumer then reads only the authorized points. A holdout is not used to choose thresholds or repaired after failure.

Current status is fail-closed: the key/recipient and coordination receipt are unprovisioned. Access gate tests exercise synthetic receipts/ciphertext only. This is materially stronger than asking an analyst not to look, but it is **not an operational seal today**. A future 109 contract must independently verify the exact CMS command/toolchain and private staging cleanup; this preflight runs no encryption on real holdout data.
