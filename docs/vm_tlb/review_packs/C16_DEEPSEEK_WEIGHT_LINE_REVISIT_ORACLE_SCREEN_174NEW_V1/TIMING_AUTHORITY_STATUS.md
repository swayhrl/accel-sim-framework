# Traffic-to-time authority

O0 is only the overly optimistic first-128B-line-touch reference: 180,224/360,448 = 50% of DeepSeek warp-record line visits could be removed under that fiction. O1 is the relevant selected 32B sector-visit proxy: 180,224/360,448 = 50%, equivalent to 5,767,168 sector bytes. O2 is exact logical BF16 read identity: 5,767,168/11,534,336 = 50%. All denominators sum **within** accepted independent static-shard replays; addresses are never unioned across shards.

All DeepSeek duplicates are inside a CTA and the very same warp/static instruction. The 50% values are *logical-work* ceilings. A 32B sector visit is not proven to be an L2 miss or a DRAM transaction. Existing accepted artifacts do not provide legal per-visit L1/L2 service identity, L2 miss attribution, DRAM bytes, or timing saved by removing these reads. `cache_hit`, `L2_miss`, `DRAM_bytes`, `TRANSLATION_TO_TIME`, and `TRAFFIC_TO_TIME` remain `UNKNOWN`. No sector count is multiplied by a fixed latency. No native performance experiment or cache mechanism follows from this screen.

Q30 independently illustrates why O1 and O2 must stay separate: its 262,144 duplicate selected sector visits have zero exact duplicate BF16 bytes. It is not a validation holdout; Q30, DeepSeek and OLMoE were all used in discovery.
