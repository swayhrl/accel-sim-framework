Across the three accepted independent model lineages, the natural-routed expert `down_proj` anchors show a shared descriptive weight/input-balanced, output-small, OTHER-zero event composition under the observed cuBLAS gemvx deployments.

## What differs materially

Executed static coverage, event-scale distributions, and per-shard spatial footprints differ; Q30 has materially fewer executed paths. Equal selected count is not static-code equivalence.

## What this does and does not justify studying next

This is descriptive, per-shard-only evidence. It does not establish matched-input causality, cross-process VA relations, cache/TLB causality, or a universal MoE law. Candidate follow-up questions require ChatGPT/user review.
