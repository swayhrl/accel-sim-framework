## Metrics and baseline identity

Let B=C_DH_OFF_matched and O=C_Oracle be positive integer cycles from the same immutable Oracle build, fixed input/ROI and original DH-P0 architecture/options, differing only in the approved Oracle enum. Then S_oracle=B/O and H=(B-O)/B=1-1/S_oracle. These are conditional comparisons. H is not a stall-cycle fraction, a critical-path decomposition, a strict/attainable bound or an additive component. It can be negative; no clipping or rejection of slower valid Oracle outcomes is allowed. With positive cycles H<1 algebraically, not as a hardware claim.

Legacy aliases keep original wrapper and underlying source identity.

A per-program best_observed accepted DH value may identify the lowest cycles actually seen among compatible tested cases. It is an exploratory selection, not a real uniform implementation or a theoretical lower cycle bound. Never average or geometrically combine these per-program best selections into a suite claim. Per-mode GM(S) uses its stated accepted OFF/Oracle intersection; direct-mode comparisons require the identical intersection. Report suites, exact integer signs, distributions and missing counts. ## Predeclared interpretation and falsification

A valid Oracle that is slower remains a negative counterfactual observation; inspect retained FIFO/service/ordering interactions rather than repairing the data. A natural exit with missing ownership/replies is not a valid counterfactual. A semantic failure holds the mode, not its inconvenient programs. Accepted zero responses do not prove no headroom outside the particular intervention. Lower request counts with higher cycles falsify a simple monotonic traffic-only explanation.
