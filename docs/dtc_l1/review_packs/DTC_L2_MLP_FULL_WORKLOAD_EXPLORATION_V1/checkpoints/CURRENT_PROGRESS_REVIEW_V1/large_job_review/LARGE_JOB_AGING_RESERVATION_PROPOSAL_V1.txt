1. Eligibility and fairness: choose at most one reservation target. Trigger after an input has waited>=30min and has a large nonfit candidate (e.g. >=8GiB), with no current model for that input. Prefer a never-started frozen program first, then oldest eligible input, then its predeclared DH-P0. At this snapshot that is Lava DH-P0. Other identities remain in the matrix. Use immutable first eligibility (initial broad qualification/registration or first-seen journal), not mutable spec mtime or a retry timestamp. No measured performance enters selection.

Feasibility guard before reserving: calculate unchanged safe budget after conservative natural release of OUR current anonymousPSS, remove only those own future-growth entries, retain peers and current external use. If target still cannot fit even after full own drain, do not throttle ordinary work. Record EXTERNAL_LIMIT_NOT_RESOLVABLE_BY_OWN_DRAIN, leave target pending and reevaluate when resource state changes. No hypothetical external release, peak reduction, or process interruption is permitted.

3. Accumulate protected credit gradually: start reserveR=0. On observed natural completion, estimate attributable released credit from its last measured anonymousPSS and withdrawn future-growth entry. Protect75%, leaving25% available for small-job mixing. Clamp new credit against actual positive safe-margin increase after reconciling new claims/observed completions, so old RSS snapshots, heap shrink or external fluctuations are not presented as already-free capacity. Keep reservation accounting in the owner-instance operational state/journal; do not edit claims semantics or registered specs. R is capped at the target's max(canonical,currentforecast).

4. Backfill condition: C = exact unchanged provider admission budget minus exact committed future growth. A normal candidate may start only if original full gates pass AND C-candidate_cost >= R. Thus backfill never spends accumulated protected credit. Existing79 jobs continue. Large-job reservation is one target, not all10 Lava points. It gradually reduces new admissions while a large collection of healthy jobs keeps running, rather than stopping them or serializing the entire campaign.

5. Target admission: when C covers the full target cost, offer the target before backfill, through the normal4-slot pre-exec and native exact prepare. Preserve its reservation across zero-start defer. Clear it only once the target's legitimate consumed/model generation is observed (its real claim then carries its forecast), or the point naturally closes; never resubmit a spent uncertain identity. Resume ordinary fitting immediately afterward. Other old large inputs such as SGEMM/scalarProd may acquire the next single reservation by the same policy.

6. Reevaluate feasibility, external pressure and target state every observer cycle, with a full anonymousPSS feasibility snapshot at most every30-60s. Existing pressure pauses still apply. If external changes make own-drain insufficient, release reservation throttling and allow normal safe fits; record the reason. Do not set a blind timeout which repeatedly discards accumulated credit and recreates starvation. Keep phase/target/R/C/actual available budget visible for review and support precise restoration of the previous chooser instance.
