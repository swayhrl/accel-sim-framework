M8 is a predeclared smallest merge increment; original M16 failure and measured speedups did not select it. These six seeds are **not a total simulation cap**. Each of the five pair seeds yields two fixed contexts, one for each changed axis, with the other parameter held at that seed's value. The wide seed yields four more contexts, holding its other three parameters fixed. Total14 contexts; no new Cartesian product. Once the wide seed has full35 accepted evidence, its four single-axis ablations are required irrespective of gain. An independent completed context may progress while unrelated seeds/contexts are pending; its actual seed and baseline-context parents must themselves be full35 accepted.

Every contextual ladder first completes1x/2x/4x values relative to that axis's P0. For example E192_F256, E384_F256, E768_F256; and E768_M4, E768_M8, E768_M16. The latter new joint trial does **not** fill original OFAT M16. A nonpositive highest marginal after a positive previous marginal requests one doubled confirmation. Two consecutive nonpositive marginals stop only that measured context; a later positive marginal resumes doubling. A single flat seed never closes every axis. Resource/evidence holds are not saturation. No preset start-count cap stops exploration. This sparse coordinate design does not prove an exhaustive or physical performance upper bound.

## Mandatory three-class uniform comparison

Besides baseline P0, define two **predeclared disjoint** non-P0 resource tiers. Moderate requires every axis <= first increment: E384/M8/Q64/F128. High-resource potential requires at least one axis above those fixed thresholds, with no arbitrary highest-level cap. Only actually completed full35 accepted uniform profiles are eligible. For each tier choose highest whole35 DH_P0/DH GM, tie by lexicographic entries/merge/miss_queue/frfcfs values, then profile name. These dimensional tie rules are deterministic and are **not a physical area model**. The same dimensional tie rule selects the overall/suite uniform champions; no raw sum of unrelated slots is treated as area.

Both tier winners require full35 CH even if they are flat or regress relative to P0; `improvement_proven=false` is reported accurately. Their disjoint resource regions guarantee different parameter vectors. If no full35 representative exists in a tier, preserve evidence HOLD and prevent adoption rather than substituting one vector for both. Global positive champion and fixed-suite useful champions join these two requirements by exact-vector deduplication. The selected high-tier point is best observed performance within its declared resource region, not merely the largest configuration and not an upper-bound proof. Existing V1 formal fields remain untouched; V3 adds explicit tier fields in `interaction_search.uniform_resource_tiers`.
