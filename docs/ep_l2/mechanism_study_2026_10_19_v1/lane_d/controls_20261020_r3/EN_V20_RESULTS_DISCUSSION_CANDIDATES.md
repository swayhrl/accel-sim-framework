# Standalone English update — four accepted Conv controls

These paragraphs supersede the r2 two-control candidate where the new diagnostics are discussed. They do not modify the EN-v20 mother or replace any main-F result.

**Results.** W_ONLY recovers 1407 of the complete scheme's 1463-cycle improvement on Conv (96.17%), with 392 cycles saved in Rows and 1015 in Columns. This ratio describes net program-time differences, not the fraction caused by copying. The traditional S_COPY_FREE diagnostic is instead 739 cycles slower than S: Rows regresses by 1815 cycles while Columns improves by 1076. This diagnostic removes copy-bank service and the fixed copy-staging delay, while retaining finite static WB organization. Its adverse result shows that removing these modeled copy costs alone does not reproduce the W-only improvement.

**Conditional mechanisms.** Removing V with RO retained increases execution time by 1994 cycles, comprising 1555 Rows and 439 Columns cycles. Removing RO with V retained increases execution time by 963 cycles, but the full scheme is 706 cycles slower in Rows and 1669 faster in Columns. F and F_MINUS_V record their new RO admissions in Rows; later Columns differences may include inherited state. Direct victim responses, lower traffic and service counts do not identify these elapsed-time effects. For example, S_COPY_FREE reduces physical bank services by 1096097 and eliminates observed copy-pipeline waiting but still runs slower.

**Discussion.** These differences are conditional net effects, rather than additive V/RO savings. S and W_ONLY use role/id/write bank-retry matching; enabling dirty V or RO also enables client/UID/generation matching. Thus W-only-to-feature comparisons include this source-derived identity change. F_MINUS_V and F retain the stronger rule, as do F_MINUS_R and F. No first-UID divergence or new correctness failure is established. Overlapping waits are not summed into GPU stalls; successful Tag detach remains zero in the four main-F runs. The original four-pair results remain unchanged, and the seven-input geometric mean is still unknown.
