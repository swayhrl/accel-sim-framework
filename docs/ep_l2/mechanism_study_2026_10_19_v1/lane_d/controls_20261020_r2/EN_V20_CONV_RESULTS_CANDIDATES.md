# Standalone English insertion candidates — two accepted Conv controls

No edits to the EN-v20 mother. These statements apply only to E-accepted full-input main61 endpoints; controls do not replace the full-F main result.

**Results.** On Conv, W_ONLY reduces execution time from 292932 to 291525 cycles, recovering 1407 of the full scheme's 1463-cycle improvement (96.17%). Its Rows and Columns kernels save 392 and 1015 cycles, respectively. The complete scheme is only 56 cycles faster overall, but it is 140 cycles slower in Rows and 196 faster in Columns. The 96.17% figure compares two net program-time differences; it is not an estimate of the fraction attributable to copy elimination.

**Conditional effects.** Disabling V while retaining W and RO increases execution time from 291469 to 293463 cycles. Of this 1994-cycle increase, 1555 cycles occur in Rows and 439 in Columns. The full scheme records only 36 direct victim-read responses, and its total lower-read traffic is only 1152 bytes smaller. Nevertheless, removing V changes RO transaction allocation and merging, as well as MSHR, MissQ and same-address-WB waits. Both conditions record all new RO admissions in Rows; the Columns difference therefore includes effects beyond newly admitted RO transactions, including possible inherited state. The observations support a conditional net benefit of V in the RO-enabled configuration, rather than a benefit attributable solely to direct victim hits or reduced traffic.

**Interpretation.** Source review shows that S and W_ONLY use role/id/write bank-retry matching, whereas RO-enabled conditions and the complete scheme additionally match client, UID and generation. Thus W_ONLY-to-F changes include retry-identity semantics alongside V and RO. F_MINUS_V and F both retain the stronger rule, so their 1994-cycle difference does not isolate an identity-rule switch. Query, retention and scheduling effects remain coupled; overlapping request waits are not summed into GPU stalls. The current four-main-pair aggregate remains an interim subset, and the original seven-input geometric mean is unknown.
