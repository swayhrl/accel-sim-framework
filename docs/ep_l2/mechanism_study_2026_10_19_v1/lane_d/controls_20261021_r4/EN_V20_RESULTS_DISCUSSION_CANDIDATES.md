# Standalone EN-v20 insertion candidates — eight accepted controls

No changes to the English mother. These paragraphs complement the retained main-results and Conv W/copy-free discussion. All numbers are E-accepted full-application measurements on main61; waits are observational and nonexclusive.

**Conv factorial results.** On the fixed WV background, either T alone, M alone, or both produces 291469 cycles, versus 292432 with both disabled. Each active corner has identical Rows/Columns durations (160984/130485), but different resource behavior: T-only incurs 371495 normal line-MSHR allocation events, whereas M-only and the full scheme avoid those allocations. The conditional cycle effect of either feature is −963 when the other is disabled and zero when it is enabled. The signed interaction, $C_{11}-C_{10}-C_{01}+C_{00}$, is therefore +963 cycles; the two individual reductions are not additive.

**Conv interpretation.** All three active corners share identical captured bank-service and lower-traffic totals and report zero successful Tag detach. Either switch also enables the common RO transaction path, so the T-only comparison cannot attribute its gain to executed Tag detachment. T-only's recorded RO-sampled line occupancy peaks remain below capacity, consistent with the extra line reservations not adding an observed delay here. Equal elapsed time does not establish functional equivalence: allocation behavior differs, and equivalence of request schedules or other workloads was not tested.

**CFD results and discussion.** With the other mechanisms retained, enabling V saves 1429 cycles and enabling RO saves 1655. These conditional gains concentrate in flux (1559 and 1690 cycles), offset by 130 and 35 cycles in preceding kernels. Yet complete F remains 64 cycles slower than traditional S: 78 earlier cycles outweigh a 14-cycle flux improvement. The remove-one backgrounds differ from S, and their gains cannot be added into independent mechanism contributions. Disabling RO even slightly reduces lower-read traffic and bank services while making execution slower; aggregate service demand therefore does not explain its conditional gain. Changes in V/RO admission, merging and WB/response ordering remain plausible explanations, rather than a measured critical-path decomposition.

**Evidence boundary.** Eight of the ten authorized control attempts are accepted; both original DWT controls ended at RAM limits and supply no complete performance result. Their later recovery episode is tracked separately. Conv's adverse copy-free result remains retained, as do all four main S/F pairs. Controls do not replace the main scheme, and the original seven-input geometric mean remains unknown. No successful application-level Tag-detach benefit is established by the current four main runs.
