# Proposed replacements for EN-v22: Results and Discussion

Editorial candidates only. EN-v22 remains the sole manuscript; no mother file is edited. Numbers below are bound to E authority ca2dbc456 and the MAIN/control identities in this pack. Replace earlier four-input positive-GM language when describing the current accepted subset; do not present either subset as the completed seven-input evaluation.

**Main results.** Across the five currently accepted complete-input pairs on the same simulator binary, the full design yields speedups of 0.502% for Convolution and 0.928% for SAD, while CFD, DWT and Vector change by −0.081%, −0.670% and −1.790%, respectively. The geometric mean of the five S/F ratios is 0.997733× (−0.227%). These measurements retain the unfavorable Vector outcome. The original seven-input aggregate remains unavailable because FWT lacks an accepted complete pair and Btree remains on hold. Diagnostic and mechanism-subset configurations do not replace the full-design endpoints in this aggregate.

**Vector: activation without reuse benefit.** Vector illustrates why fewer local operations need not improve program time. The full design eliminates 100,568 copy reads and the same number of copy writes and reduces physical bank grants by 7.17%, yet runtime increases from 73,696 to 75,039 cycles. Victim queries reach 375,000 with no actual Victim read response or promotion. The RO path admits 1,000,000 requests into 250,118 transactions and performs 749,882 merges, but total lower read traffic remains 32 MB in both configurations. Successful Tag detach is zero. The named kernel accounts for a 1,371-cycle increase, offset by a 28-cycle reduction in the final residual. Query and landing observations motivate a control-overhead and retention-timing hypothesis; the available snapshots cannot identify the critical requests or assign the 1,343-cycle net regression to a unique mechanism.

**DWT: a conditional effect extending beyond the active RO stage.** DWT completes in 166,519, 166,792, 166,718 and 167,643 cycles for S, W_ONLY, WV and F, respectively. Enabling the RO path in the W+V background therefore adds 925 cycles. Kernels 5 and 8 add 694 and 384 cycles; kernel 1 saves 91 cycles and the remaining kernels save 62. All 39,836 RO admissions and 9,965 avoided line-MSHR allocation events occur in kernel 1, with no new RO admission or query in kernels 5 and 8. The adverse conditional effect thus includes state and timing consequences that cannot be described as direct RO lookup stalls in the later kernels. It does not identify separate, additive Tag-detach and MSHR-bypass contributions. Both sides retain the strict retry-identity rule, whereas the W_ONLY-to-WV comparison also changes its activation.

**Conv: a finite-residual diagnostic, not a hardware bound.** Exempting modeled L2 data service changes Convolution's S endpoint by +599 cycles, comprising +985 in Rows and −386 in Columns. The analogous F diagnostic changes runtime by −149 cycles, comprising −786 and +637. Physical bank grants and data-service waits are exempted, while finite metadata and storage resources, real completion paths and S's fixed copy latch remain. F's slower Columns stage coincides with increased line-MSHR, same-address-WB and landing wait observations and higher lower-level WB traffic. These observations support investigating resource-acquisition and prior-state timing effects, but overlapping request waits cannot be summed into program stalls. The diagnostic is neither a hardware performance upper bound nor evidence that physical bank service is universally noncritical.

**Scope of mechanism claims.** All five accepted full-design applications record zero successful Tag detach. Consequently, their performance cannot be presented as measured benefit from executed Tag detachment. RO admission, merging and allocation avoidance are exercised, but their counts alone do not establish critical-path relief. Likewise, the Conv W_ONLY endpoint reproducing 1,407 of the full design's 1,463 saved cycles describes a net conditional performance correspondence, not a copy-stall fraction. Existing adverse controls, CFD's positive removal contrasts alongside its slightly slower full design, and SAD's unexplained zero-copy-prefix improvement remain part of the evidence.
