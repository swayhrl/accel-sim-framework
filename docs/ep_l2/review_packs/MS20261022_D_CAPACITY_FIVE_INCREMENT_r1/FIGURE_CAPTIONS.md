# Figure and table captions for separate diagnostic evidence

**CAPACITY_FIVE_PERFORMANCE.** Same-new-binary OFF/ON speedup, 100×(OFF/ON−1), over five fixed diagnostic inputs and their unweighted geometric mean. CFD is retained below zero. All ON classifications remain UNKNOWN. The joint capacity/data-service diagnostic and its GM are separate from main61 MAIN and the incomplete original-seven aggregate.

**CONV_CAPACITY_PHASES.** Conv's accepted named-kernel durations, signed ON−OFF elapsed differences including final residual, and eliminated lower-interface bytes (decimal MB). The column kernel contributes 90.61% of elapsed reduction; similar removed read-byte counts in the two kernels do not establish similar causal savings. Mechanism snapshot differences are adjacent cumulative observations, not exclusive stalls.

**CAPACITY_FIVE_STAGE_DELTAS.** All accepted elapsed phase differences for the five inputs, including negative and adverse tiny values and the reported final program residual. Per-row phase differences sum to the accepted whole-program difference. Axes have independent ranges; the plot does not sum overlapping wait counters or attribute stages to mechanisms.

**CAPACITY_FIVE_LOWER_TRAFFIC.** Measured lower-interface read and WB bytes (decimal MB) at application completion for each accepted OFF/ON pair. This is issue/service accounting, not DRAM bandwidth, a required dirty-cache flush, or numerical output validation. Small SAD values are retained exactly in the CSV despite the common scale.

**CAPACITY_FIVE_TABLE.** Exact whole-application cycles, OFF/ON ratio, UNKNOWN classification, and the fixed-five diagnostic GM. It must retain its diagnostic caption and may not be inserted as a MAIN S/F table.
