## Merge decisions

The manuscript cites twelve of fourteen preserved BibTeX keys. `mrpb` and `victim` remain in the R20 library but are omitted from this WB-only manuscript because its former short L1/victim comparison was removed. Optional TVD/RO qualification limits remain in the design section. There is one BibTeX pipeline, no duplicate inline bibliography; actual first-citation order is checked against the generated `.bbl`.

The EP-L2 distinction is our source/evidence-bound research scope: reuse a resident tag while its victim retains the same independently owned slot, secure a separate legal incoming landing, and release local payload and WAD ordering at different events. The cited papers support the prior-art descriptions; they do not prove exhaustive novelty or validate this protocol. Existing Lane6 claim/source and lifecycle audits remain authoritative for implementation mapping.

Q4 is buffering, credit and scheduling-visibility sensitivity. D4 retains Q4 buffering and raises the DRAM domain frequency; unchanged integer timing parameters shorten physical duration while the fixed 100-GPU-cycle interface delay remains. This interpretation follows the r19 contract and effective configuration, not a result claimed by SMS. No result is filled from literature, queue-event counts, or a running simulator.
