## Performance claims that the experiment supports

Conv A/B/C cycles 362791/367152/364463 prove B increases cycles 1.2021% versus A; C reduces cycles 0.7324% versus B, but still increases cycles 0.4609% versus A. Thus the correction improves legacy T in this setting, while corrected T still has no net end-to-end benefit over F-minus-T. More detaches (237->360) are not evidence that T itself accelerates this input.

Rows C-B=+654, Columns C-B=-3308 and the final residual C-B=-35 sum to -2689. This is timing localization. Neither a per-detach causal attribution nor a claim that the residual is pure drain is supported. The modeled 2954 query slice-exposures cannot be subtracted from GPU cycles: slices can act in parallel and requests can overlap other work. The same restriction applies to Bank, WAD, MissQ, LowerQ, credit and scheduler accumulated waits. Whether a particular exposure lies on the GPU critical path is UNKNOWN.

GEMM S/W/WV=558588/558957/550642 cycles; FWT=181827/181635/178951. W's changes are nearly neutral on these inputs. Adding the V package to W reduces cycles approximately 1.48% in each. That package result is PROVEN. The causal contribution of each individual V hit, dirty forwarding, retention decision, metadata lookup or changed queue ordering is UNKNOWN. Victim responses are sectors/request responses, not independently avoided DRAM transactions. Lower successful bytes can be compared only using their own same-unit population; they cannot be inferred by counting V responses.

Completed write bytes are measured during the commandlist plus hardware drain. They are not the write bytes under a hypothetical forced flush of every dirty resident/Victim line. Lower measured write traffic does not establish permanent elimination of program stores.
