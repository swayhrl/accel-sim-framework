## Precise lower-memory event boundary

C7E successful_* bytes/issues are emitted at L2-to-DRAM FIFO dequeue into the fixed-delay queue, before actual MCpush. Their read/write naming actually distinguishes return-required requests from no-return L1/L2 WB. The current WRITE_BACK/LAZY_FETCH configuration supports ordinary read/WB interpretation, but does not replace producer classes with a packet-type census. Kernel shared deltas describe admission time intervals, not DRAM completion or originating-kernel ownership.

Likewise EPL2DRAMV1 bandwidth_util is a nominal admitted-payload load ratio, not direct physical DRAM bus utilization. Whole-application hardware drain supports completion of the issued population, but does not change event timing or force every final dirty resident/Victim line to memory. Thus the accepted counter arithmetic and cycle results remain intact while paper labels must be precise. No physical-bandwidth or permanent-store-elimination claim is made from these counters.
