## Work reduction and the unresolved capacity contribution

Unified removes the explicit copy work, but the resulting work reduction is much larger than the observed throughput gain. At H0, Convolution performs 9,217,033 logical payload RAM operations with B16 and 8,124,070 with Unified. Two RAM operations for each of B16's 546,865 copied lines account for 1,093,730 operations, close to the observed difference of 1,092,963; the residual reflects other schedule and traffic changes. Bank0 carries31.26% of B16 work versus24.80% with Unified. These are work and placement measurements, not an execution-time decomposition. Unified reaches local WB-record peaks55 and37 in Convolution and FWT, respectively, but using more than16 records does not establish their marginal performance value. The same-binary S64 and U_CAP16 application comparisons remain pending. S64 redistributes the fixed1168-slot budget to1024 resident,80 bypass and64 WB slots; U_CAP16 preserves Unified organization while bounding local reserved-plus-live WB obligations at16. Comparing U_CAP16 with B16 therefore does not isolate copying alone.

## Completion control changes the effect of downstream relief

In Q4, Convolution has no B16 WB-data-full events, yet WAD reaches its128-entry limit and the DRAM scheduler continues to reject issue attempts. B16/Unified cycles are290,333/293,709. Increasing only WAD metadata capacity to256 under the same Q4 background removes the observed WAD-full events and changes the pair to292,132/289,899, shifting B/U from0.98851 to1.00770. Scheduler refusals remain nonzero, and B16 itself becomes slower. This supports an interaction between completion-control capacity and organization; it does not equate retry counts with saved cycles or establish superiority to a stronger static organization in this condition. Data storage released at lower acceptance and WAD responsibility retained until completion are separate resources. The corresponding strong-static, capacity-ablation and additional-workload evidence is still required for a broader claim.

For vectorAdd, the local-diagnostic release gives73,696 cycles at B16_OFF,73,843 at I_WB,73,696 at I_META and43,020 at I_READ_HIT. I_WB eliminates observed data-capacity refusals without improving throughput; the demand-read service relaxation is substantially more effective in this input. Separately, the combined release gives73,696 cycles at its own OFF anchor and17,247 at ALL_DATA, or4.273× operational headroom. Each comparison uses its own same-release anchor. The two OFF counts happen to match, but the releases differ, so these observations do not form a single-source decomposition of local and global opportunity. ALL_DATA retains the native instruction stream, producer ordering and architectural consumption constraints; its byte-address data evidence is symbolic, not numerical SASS output. It is neither an EP-L2 speedup nor a strict bound for arbitrary schedules. Matched Convolution/FWT local/full-diagnostic groups remain pending.

## Negative controls and causal scope

CFD remains a negative control: Unified reduces logical RAM work from1,638,275 to1,574,347 but increases application cycles from79,093 to79,262. D4 Convolution similarly has fewer RAM operations and bank waits with Unified while taking1,023 more cycles. A new small real-cache test demonstrates that a pending replacement head can block an otherwise ready hit to a different bank; it also checks stable victim identity and subsequent progress. This establishes the policy's existence, not its exclusive contribution to application critical paths. Kernel shared-resource intervals were checked for overlap and retained as shared deltas; retry events, operation waits and overlapping resource observations are not added as exclusive execution time.
